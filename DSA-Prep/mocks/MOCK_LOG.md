# Mock Interview Log

10 timed mocks across the 12 weeks. I play the interviewer: cold problem, no hints for the first 10 minutes, I question every complexity claim, I dry-run your code on an adversarial input, and I always ask a follow-up variant.

**Format:** 45 minutes unless noted. You share your thinking out loud throughout. I score the four things real interviewers score.

## Scoring rubric (1–4 each; 3 = hire bar at SDE2)

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem solving** | needed the approach given | found it with heavy hints | found it with a nudge | found it independently, considered alternatives |
| **Coding** | didn't compile / major bugs | worked after several fixes | clean, minor bugs self-caught | clean, correct first pass, good naming |
| **Communication** | went silent, jumped to code | narrated some | clarified, narrated, checked in before coding | interviewer always knew the plan; drove the conversation |
| **Verification** | declared done untested | tested after being asked | dry-ran unprompted, found own bugs | tested edge cases systematically, correct complexity stated |
